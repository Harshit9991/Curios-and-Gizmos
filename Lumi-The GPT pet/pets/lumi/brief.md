# Lumi — frozen animation concepts

Name: Lumi. Cute anime moon fox, quadrupedal anatomy, cream fur, navy ear tips, teal eyes, fluffy tail, navy cape and gold star clasp.

1. idle: natural fox sit, front paws planted, hind legs tucked; breathing, blinking, slow fluffy tail sway.
2. running-right: four-legged bounding trot toward screen-right.
3. running-left: matching four-legged bounding trot toward screen-left.
4. waving: lift one front paw, ears perk up.
5. jumping: crouch, spring upward, land softly at original baseline.
6. failed: ears droop, eyes close, head lowers, readable sad emotion, gentle recovery.
7. waiting: curious expectant head tilt, one front paw slightly raised.
8. running (active work): animal sitting posture, paws tucked; eyes closed, occasional ear twitch; meditate and levitate with low, slow vertical hovering. Preserve animal anatomy, no human cross-legged pose.
9. review: lean forward, scan thoughtfully, tiny nod.

Sixteen look directions: head and gaze turn naturally while body and paws remain anchored.

Production: generate coherent frames, inspect each loop, assemble nine standard rows, generate and inspect sixteen look directions, validate exact final spritesheet, preview motion, then create and select Lumi. No decorative floating particles, symbols, shadows or motion lines.

## Approved refinement

Remove purple chroma fringe from all animations. Only the meditating levitation state has a faint purple aura, slightly thicker than the original fringe. Keep it attached closely to the silhouette with no loose particles. Draft playback timing is approximately 15–17% faster than the first previews; state concepts remain unchanged.
